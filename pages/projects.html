Projects from my chemical engineering coursework, from a protein production poster to an instrumentation experiment to a full plant design.

Lithium dihydrogen phosphate plant design

CHEE 456 Design Project · Team of four · Fall 2026

My design team is evaluating a plant that would produce 30,000 tonnes per year of battery-grade lithium dihydrogen phosphate (LDP), a precursor for LFP and LMFP battery cathodes, from the lithium-rich eluate of a direct lithium extraction process. We compare production routes and evaluate the process steps, aiming to maximize lithium recovery while minimizing costs and waste.

The role of kinesin in biotechnology

CHEE 370 Elements of Biotechnology · Team of four · Fall 2024

With three teammates, I produced a research poster on kinesin, the ATP-powered motor protein that moves cargo along microtubules. It covers the protein's structure and function, its regulation and applications in cell biology, cancer therapy and nanotechnology, as well as how kinesin is produced.

Calibrating a thermistor to measure cooling water

CHEE 291 Instrumentation and Measurement · Fall 2023

I built a temperature sensor from a 10 kΩ thermistor, a voltage divider and an Arduino Uno, then calibrated it against ice, tap and boiling water baths. I used the sensor to record cooling water and compared the results with Newton's law of cooling.
