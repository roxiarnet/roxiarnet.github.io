Research and industry

Helix Carbon

Research intern · The Engine at MIT · May to Aug. 2026

Helix Carbon is a cleantech startup building electrochemical systems that convert CO₂ into syngas, a mix of CO and H₂ that feeds into fuels and chemicals. I worked on improving CO selectivity in a cation-exchange-membrane CO₂ electrolyzer by suppressing proton crossover to the cathode. I fabricated and tested modified catalyst layers, ionomer coatings and a buffering separator layer, and the best configuration roughly tripled CO selectivity over baseline with stable performance over multi-hour runs. I wrote the final technical report on the project.

Nth Cycle

Chemical engineering intern · Burlington, MA · May to Aug. 2025

Nth Cycle develops electro-extraction technology for recovering critical metals. I operated flow-through electro-extraction cells at benchtop, pilot and commercial scale, covering start-up, sampling, shutdown and performance analysis. I led benchtop clean-in-place testing to address solid buildup in the cells, analyzed samples by ICP-OES to report recoveries and component balances, and operated a filter press.

Planck Energies

Summer research intern · Radiative cooling · May to Aug. 2024

I worked on passive radiative cooling paint, which sheds heat to the sky without using electricity. I synthesized calcium phosphate for a cooling pigment, formulated water-based paints with lower VOC content, and ran large-scale outdoor tests against commercial white paint. I also built a temperature-monitoring system with a Raspberry Pi and a real-time IoT dashboard.

Hydrate Tech Lab

Undergraduate research assistant · McGill University · Sept. 2025 to Jan. 2026

In McGill's Hydrate Tech Lab I investigated crystalline nanocellulose as a promoter in a liquid-to-solid phase change system, with implications for gas hydrate formation and energy storage. I ran experiments on nucleation kinetics, stability and efficiency, and analyzed the results for possible use in large-scale hydrate technologies.

McGill ChemE Car

Technical co-captain · previously mechanical lead · Sept. 2023 to present

The team designs and builds a small car powered by a chemical reaction that has to carry a load and stop at a distance announced on competition day. I oversee both the chassis and the chemistry, including our Zn/Cu and Mg-Cu gel batteries and a stopping mechanism based on magnesium dissolving in sulfuric acid, and I run CAD and 3D printing workshops for new members. We competed at ChemE Car Nationals in Boston in Fall 2025.
